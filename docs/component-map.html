The Cut

“Your designer will walk you through pricing on the consultation, because it depends on your site and your choices.”

Nikhil asked for per-sq-ft quoting. The agent does not do it. A price in the studio’s voice is heard as a commitment and cannot be withdrawn — so it is blocked three times, because a prompt is a request, not a guarantee.

  1. The rate card is never in the room. Only qualified.md and services.md load. lib/knowledge.ts refuses to start if pricing.md ever appears.
  2. The output is filtered in code. Rupee amounts, lakh and crore figures, per-sq-ft rates and estimate language are stripped from every free-text field. A budget the caller volunteered survives — it is their statement, and the designer needs it.
  3. Asking can never disqualify. A not_fit that cites pricing, with no volunteered budget, is overruled in code and the override is recorded on the row.
